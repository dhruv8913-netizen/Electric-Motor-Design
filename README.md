# Electric Motor CAD Model | SolidWorks

## Overview

This repository contains the 3D CAD model and design documentation of an electric motor developed in SolidWorks. The project demonstrates the modeling and assembly of key motor components, emphasizing dimensional accuracy, component integration, and engineering best practices for electric vehicle applications.

## Objectives

* Develop a detailed 3D model of an electric motor assembly.
* Accurately model major motor components and their interfaces.
* Ensure proper alignment and fit between rotating and stationary parts.
* Create a design suitable for visualization, integration, and engineering documentation.
* Apply CAD best practices for assembly modeling and manufacturability.

## Design Features

* Complete motor assembly modeled in SolidWorks.
* Detailed components including the housing, rotor, stator, shaft, end covers, and fasteners.
* Parametric design for easy modification of dimensions.
* Accurate assembly constraints and component positioning.
* Engineering drawings for key parts and assemblies.
* Designed for integration into larger mechanical or electric vehicle systems.
